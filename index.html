Koha Children's Holds Queue Reformatter

Instructions

  1. Open the Koha Circulation module and select "Holds queue"
  2. Leave the collection dropdown set as "All" and click "Submit"
  3. Leave all default columns visible. Click "Export" and select "Excel"
  4. On this page, click the "Browse..." or "Choose File" button, locate the exported spreadsheet, and then press "Process Spreadsheet"
  5. Once the reformatted table is displayed, click the green "Print" button. It is likely best to print as a double-sided landscape page.
